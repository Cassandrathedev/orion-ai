Our Story

We built the tool we always wished existed

In 2023, our founding team was running a fast-moving startup and spending hours every week on manual handoffs between tools. We tried every automation platform out there and they were either too complex, too limited, or too expensive to scale.

So we built Orion. A platform that combines the simplicity of no-code automation with the intelligence of AI, designed for teams who need to move fast without breaking things.

Today, Orion powers thousands of teams across the world, from two-person startups to Fortune 500 engineering departments. And we're just getting started.

✦ Our Values

What we stand for

⚡

Speed matters

Every feature we ship is designed to save time. If something slows your team down, we haven't done our job. Speed is a feature, not a nice-to-have.

🔍

Radical transparency

We're honest about what Orion can and can't do. No marketing smoke and mirrors. No vague promises. Just clear, accurate communication — always.

🤝

Built on trust

Your data is yours. We'll never sell it, share it without consent, or use it to train our models. Security and privacy are non-negotiable at Orion.

🌱

Long-term thinking

We're building Orion to last. That means saying no to shortcuts, investing in infrastructure, and making decisions that serve our customers in year five, not just this quarter.
